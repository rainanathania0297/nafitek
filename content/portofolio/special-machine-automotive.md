# Custom Assembly Machine for Automotive Parts Manufacturer

## Client Overview
PT Otomotif Prima is a tier-1 supplier to major automotive manufacturers, specializing in sensor components for vehicle safety systems. With increasing demand for their products, they needed to significantly increase production capacity while maintaining strict quality standards.

## Challenge
The client faced several challenges with their existing manual assembly process:

- Limited production capacity unable to meet growing demand
- Inconsistent assembly quality leading to high rejection rates
- Labor-intensive process requiring skilled operators
- Difficulty maintaining consistent production pace
- Quality documentation requirements from automotive customers
- Need for improved traceability for safety-critical components

## Solution
PT Nafitek Global Indonesia designed and built a custom assembly machine tailored to the client's specific requirements:

### Machine Design
- Fully automated 8-station rotary assembly system
- Precision component feeding and orientation systems
- Multi-stage assembly process with verification at each step
- Integrated testing station for electrical performance
- Laser marking system for traceability
- Automatic good/reject part sorting

### Control System
- Allen-Bradley CompactLogix PLC control system
- Touchscreen HMI with intuitive operator interface
- Recipe management for different product variants
- Comprehensive data collection for quality records
- OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) monitoring
- Integration with factory MES system

### Quality Features
- Vision inspection at critical assembly stages
- Force monitoring for press-fit operations
- Electrical testing of completed assemblies
- 100% traceability with serialization
- Statistical process control (SPC) data collection
- Automatic rejection of non-conforming products

## Implementation Process
1. **Requirements Analysis**: Detailed assessment of product specifications and production needs
2. **Conceptual Design**: Development of machine concept and approval by client
3. **Detailed Engineering**: Mechanical, electrical, and software design
4. **Manufacturing**: Fabrication of custom components and assembly of machine
5. **Factory Acceptance Testing**: Comprehensive testing at our facility
6. **Installation**: On-site installation and integration
7. **Validation**: Process validation according to automotive industry standards
8. **Training**: Operator and maintenance training
9. **Production Support**: Initial production ramp-up assistance

## Results
The custom assembly machine delivered exceptional results:

- **200% increase** in production capacity
- **Rejection rate reduced** from 3.5% to less than 0.2%
- **Labor requirement reduced** by 75% for the assembly process
- **100% traceability** for every component assembled
- **Consistent quality** meeting automotive industry standards
- **Flexible production** with quick changeover between variants
- **ROI achieved** within 12 months of operation
