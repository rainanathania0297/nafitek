# PT Nafitek Global Indonesia - Company Profile Website Requirements

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** PT Nafitek Global Indonesia Company Profile Website  
**Framework:** Nuxt 3 (Static Site Generation)  
**Project Type:** Static Company Profile Website  
**Target Audience:** Potential clients, partners, and stakeholders in the machinery industry  

## Business Context

PT Nafitek Global Indonesia is a machinery procurement, maintenance, and repair services company established in January 2018. The company specializes in:
- High-quality machinery spare parts supply
- Special purpose machine manufacturing
- Maintenance and repair services
- Automation services
- Distribution of various industrial brands

## Website Structure & Pages

### 1. Homepage (/)
**Purpose:** Company introduction and main value proposition
**Content Requirements:**
- Hero section with company tagline and CTA
- Brief company overview
- Key services highlights
- Company strengths showcase
- Call-to-action for contact

### 2. About Us (/about)
**Purpose:** Detailed company information and values
**Content Requirements:**
- Company history and background
- Vision and Mission statements
- Company values and culture
- Team information (if available)
- Company achievements and milestones

### 3. Services (/services)
**Purpose:** Detailed service offerings
**Content Requirements:**
- **Supply of Standard Components**
  - Mechanical and electrical spare parts
  - Vacuum accessory components
- **Special Purpose Machine**
  - Custom machine manufacturing
  - Technical specifications
- **Maintenance and Repair Services**
  - Vacuum pump specialization
  - Helium leak testing
  - Electric motor rewinding
  - Electronic component repair
- **Automation Services**
  - PLC program installation/modification
  - Electrical wiring for control panels
  - Andon systems
- **Distribution Services**
  - ATLAS COPCO vacuum pumps
  - TRUSCO products
  - Other trusted brands

### 4. Products (/products)
**Purpose:** Showcase product catalog and brands
**Content Requirements:**
- Vacuum pump brands (Pfeiffer, Leybold, Inficon, Ulvac, Shinko Seiki)
- Component categories
- Product specifications
- Brand partnerships

### 5. Contact (/contact)
**Purpose:** Contact information and lead generation
**Content Requirements:**
- Company address and contact details
- Contact form
- Office hours
- Service areas
- Map integration
